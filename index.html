Clone Voices Without ElevenLabs

Pocket TTS and Chatterbox: two free, open-source tools for custom AI voiceovers, running on your own computer.

Adi 2 min read  ·  October 2026

ElevenLabs makes voice cloning easy, but it comes with a subscription. Its Starter plan is $6/month for instant voice cloning and 30,000 credits; Creator is $22/month with professional voice cloning and 121,000 credits. The free plan has 10,000 credits but no voice cloning. Those are regular monthly prices before tax, excluding first-month offers.

↗ ElevenLabs pricing · checked October 2026

These two alternatives clone a voice from reference audio and turn your script into speech. Both are free to run locally, with no subscription or per-character fee. You provide the computer and electricity.

01 / 02

Pocket TTS

Kyutai’s lightweight option is the easiest starting point for most reasonably recent laptops and desktops. It runs on CPU, uses just two CPU cores, and needs no dedicated GPU. Give it a clean voice sample and your script; it generates a WAV voiceover in that voice.

The standard model has 100 million parameters, with smaller English models available for weaker machines. Windows, macOS and Linux are supported. Actual speed depends on your CPU and chosen model.

Chatterbox

Resemble AI’s voice-cloning family offers more expressive controls and multilingual options. Nano and Turbo support cues such as [laugh] and [chuckle]; the separate Multilingual model supports 23 languages.

For an ordinary computer without a dedicated GPU, choose Chatterbox Nano: its 110-million-parameter model is designed for CPU use. Resemble reports faster-than-real-time generation on eight CPU cores. Turbo and Multilingual are heavier; an NVIDIA CUDA GPU is the practical choice for fast generation. You don’t need one to start with Nano or Pocket TTS.

Let your agent set it up

Prepare a clean, single-speaker voice clip—about 10 seconds is a useful starting point—and your script. Use your own voice or one you have permission to clone. Paste this into your coding agent:

AGENT PROMPT 
Set up voice cloning on my computer using these official repos:
https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts
https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox

Check my OS, RAM, CPU and GPU, then read the current setup docs. Use Pocket TTS for a simple CPU setup, Chatterbox Nano for expressive English on CPU, or a larger Chatterbox model if my hardware supports it. Install in an isolated environment with dependencies matched to my machine.

I will provide reference.wav (a voice I have permission to clone) and script.txt. Generate a short sample using that voice, then export the full script as voiceover.wav. Build a simple local interface to upload the reference, enter text, listen and download WAV. Give me one command to run it again. Keep the files local.
